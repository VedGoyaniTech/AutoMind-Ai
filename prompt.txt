Yes — exactly. You want the answer UI to **automatically choose the best visual format based on the question and the answer**, not always use cards.

For example:

| -------------------------- | --------------------------- |

| “Safest 7-seater cars”     | **Ranked table + cards**    |

So the right architecture is **dynamic answer rendering**.

### The key idea

You want **one answer engine, but many renderers**:

```text
                         User Question
                              ↓
                       Intent Detection
                              ↓
                    Answer Type / Structure
                              ↓
        ┌──────────┬──────────┬──────────┬──────────┐
        ↓          ↓          ↓          ↓          ↓
      Table    Comparison    Cards      Steps     Text
        ↓          ↓          ↓          ↓          ↓
     <Table>   <Compare>   <Cards>    <Steps>    <Text>
````

So the AI isn't just deciding **what to say** — it also returns a **structured representation of what it's saying**, and your frontend decides how to visually present it. This will be much more reliable than trying to make one Markdown card handle every type of question.
