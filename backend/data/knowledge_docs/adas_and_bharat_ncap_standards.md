# Bharat NCAP Crash Safety Protocols & Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS)

## Bharat NCAP (B-NCAP) Safety Testing Protocols
Launched under AIS-197 standard by MoRTH in late 2023, Bharat NCAP evaluates passenger vehicle crash safety across three primary pillars:
1. **Adult Occupant Protection (AOP) - Max 32 Points:** Frontal offset deformable barrier test (64 km/h), side movable deformable barrier test (50 km/h), and side pole impact test (29 km/h).
2. **Child Occupant Protection (COP) - Max 49 Points:** Dynamic child restraint system (ISOFIX) testing using 18-month and 3-year crash dummies.
3. **Safety Assist Technologies (SAT):** Mandatory Electronic Stability Control (ESC), ISOFIX anchors, front seatbelt reminders, and pedestrian protection.

## Level 1 vs Level 2 ADAS Architecture
- **Level 1 ADAS:** Single-axis assistance (e.g. Adaptive Cruise Control OR Lane Keep Assist independently).
- **Level 2 ADAS:** Simultaneous longitudinal and lateral control combining Forward Collision Warning (FCW), Autonomous Emergency Braking (AEB), Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop & Go, Lane Keep Assist (LKA), Blind Spot Detection (BSD), and High Beam Assist.
- Prominent Indian 5-Star B-NCAP & Level 2 ADAS vehicles: Tata Safari, Tata Harrier, Tata Nexon, Mahindra XUV700, Hyundai Creta Facelift.
