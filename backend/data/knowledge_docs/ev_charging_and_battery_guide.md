# Comprehensive Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging & Battery Care Guide for India

## EV Charging Standards & Protocols in India
In India, passenger electric vehicles primarily use the **CCS2 (Combined Charging System Type 2)** standard for DC fast charging and the **Type 2 AC (Mennekes)** connector for standard AC charging.

### AC Charging Levels (Home & Workplace)
1. **Portable 3.3 kW AC Slow Charger (16A Socket):**
   - Typically included with vehicle purchase.
   - Charges a standard 30–45 kWh battery pack from 10% to 100% in 10 to 14 hours.
   - Recommended for overnight home charging.
2. **7.2 kW to 11 kW AC Wallbox Charger:**
   - Dedicated EV wallbox installation requiring 3-phase or high-load single-phase meter.
   - Charges a 45 kWh battery pack in 6 to 7 hours.

### DC Fast Charging on Indian Highways
- **50 kW to 60 kW DC Fast Chargers:** Available along major highways (Tata Power EZ Charge, Jio-bp pulse, Statiq, Zeon, ChargeZone).
- 10% to 80% state of charge (SoC) is achieved in approximately 45 to 55 minutes.
- **Charging Curve:** Charging speed peaks up to 80% SoC, after which the Battery Management System (BMS) automatically throttles current to protect battery chemistry.

## Battery Chemistry, Thermal Management & Longevity
- **LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) Cells:** Used by Tata Motors (Nexon EV, Punch EV, Curvv EV) and BYD (Blade Battery). Exceptional thermal stability in hot Indian summers up to 50°C.
- **Liquid Cooling vs Air Cooling:** All modern Indian four-wheeler EVs use active liquid coolant loops to maintain optimal cell temperatures between 25°C and 35°C.
- **Standard Battery Warranty:** Indian OEMs provide a standard **8 Years or 1,60,000 km battery and motor warranty** guaranteeing at least 70–80% retained capacity.
