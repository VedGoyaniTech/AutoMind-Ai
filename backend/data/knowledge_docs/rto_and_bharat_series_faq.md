# India RTO Taxes, BH Series Registration & Electric Vehicle Concessions

## Bharat Series (BH) Vehicle Registration Explained
The Bharat Series (BH) vehicle registration is designed to eliminate the need for transferring vehicle registration and paying double road taxes when an employee relocates between Indian states.

### Eligibility Criteria for BH Series
1. Defense personnel and armed forces.
2. Central and State Government employees.
3. Employees of private sector companies having registered offices in at least 4 or more Indian States or Union Territories (requires Form 60 / Working Certificate).

### BH Series Road Tax Slab Structure (Every 2 Years)
- **Vehicles below ₹10 Lakh Ex-Showroom:** 8% tax (paid in biennial installments of ~2%).
- **Vehicles ₹10 Lakh to ₹20 Lakh Ex-Showroom:** 10% tax.
- **Vehicles above ₹20 Lakh Ex-Showroom:** 12% tax.
- **Diesel Vehicles:** +2% additional surcharge on base slab.
- **Electric Vehicles:** 2% discount (-2%) on base slab.

## State-Wise Electric Vehicle (EV) Road Tax Exemptions
- **Gujarat, Maharashtra, Delhi, Rajasthan, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh:** Provide 0% to 100% waiver on RTO registration tax for battery electric passenger vehicles to accelerate zero-emission adoption.
